DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT — paraphrase prepared for the Isogeny Evidence Lab seed; not a specific advisory. It describes a well-known class of issues to exercise the research workflow.

Background. Signature verification code for RSA PKCS#1 v1.5 has repeatedly suffered from lenient parsing of the encoded message: implementations that do not check the full padding string, the DigestInfo structure, or the exact length can accept forged signatures, most famously for small public exponents (the e=3 forgery reported by Bleichenbacher in 2006 and later variants in multiple libraries). Any verifier for RSA-2048 PKCS#1 v1.5 should be tested with malformed padding, truncated signatures and signatures with trailing garbage. Libraries including older mbedTLS and OpenSSL branches have shipped fixes for variants of this class; teams should confirm the exact version in use has the fixes applied and add rejection tests for malformed inputs to their firmware update verification harness.
