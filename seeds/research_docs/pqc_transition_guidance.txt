DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT — paraphrase prepared for the Isogeny Evidence Lab seed. It summarises publicly stated transition guidance; consult the primary sources (NIST IR 8547 initial public draft, November 2024; FIPS 204; NIST SP 800-208; CNSA 2.0) before acting.

Summary. NIST's draft transition guidance proposes that digital signature algorithms with 112-bit classical security, including RSA-2048 and ECDSA with P-224-class parameters, be deprecated after 2030 and that all quantum-vulnerable digital signature algorithms, including RSA and ECDSA at every parameter size, be disallowed after 2035. Products whose firmware must remain verifiable past 2035 are therefore affected by this timeline regardless of the library used. mbedTLS 2.16 does not implement any post-quantum signature algorithm. ML-DSA (FIPS 204) and the stateful hash-based schemes LMS/HSS and XMSS (SP 800-208) are the standardised options for firmware signing; CNSA 2.0 lists LMS and XMSS as preferred for software and firmware signing and sets its own timelines. The practical attack on RSA-2048 by a quantum computer has not been demonstrated; published resource estimates vary by orders of magnitude and no date is given here. A deprecation is a policy statement, not evidence of a break.
